02Publications

Hemera: a Multi-Modal Foundation Model for the Global Ionospheric State

Paper in preparation · Work in progress

6 data sources, 16 years fused into one 89M-parameter transformer

A foundation model for the ionosphere in the spirit of modern weather models. A Perceiver-style transformer fuses ionosphere maps, ground and slant GNSS, satellite altimetry, radio occultation and space-weather indices into one latent state of the global ionosphere, rolls it forward in 24 h steps and decodes any observable at any position. Pretrained by predicting observations it was not shown, then fine-tuned for analysis and forecasting.

Probabilistic Machine Learning for Slant Total Electron Content Modelling based on GNSS

Submitted to JGR: Machine Learning and Computation · Preprint

24.5% lower single-frequency positioning error than with the IGS global ionosphere maps

Predicts the slant total electron content (STEC) of every GNSS observation directly, without the thin-shell mapping functions behind standard ionosphere maps. A residual MLP with a variational Bayesian output layer is pretrained on eleven years of GNSS data and fine-tuned for each day, and returns an uncertainty for every observation. At held-out stations it beats the IGS maps on 96% of test days, most clearly at low elevations.

Preprint Code (GitHub)

Fusion of GNSS and VLBI Data for Global Ionospheric Mapping

IAG – Geodesy for a Changing Environment 2025

≈50% lower median residuals than GNSS-only maps near VLBI sites, checked against Jason-3 altimetry

Machine learning–based data fusion framework combining dense GNSS-derived VTEC with sparse but complementary VLBI observations. Two fusion strategies are investigated: direct assimilation of station-based VLBI VTEC and end-to-end integration of VLBI differential TEC (DTEC). A weighted loss balances heterogeneous data volumes, with evaluation against independent Jason-3 altimetry measurements over a full year.

Springer Poster (PDF) Code (GitHub)

A Comparative Study of VTEC Estimates Derived from S/X VLBI and VGOS Observations

AGU Space Weather · Code + data available

4.2 vs 5.6 TECU RMS to GIM under high solar activity: VGOS stays more stable than S/X VLBI

Comparative analysis of vertical total electron content (VTEC) estimates derived from colocated S/X VLBI and VGOS observations during simultaneous sessions. Results show good agreement with GNSS-based Global Ionosphere Maps (GIM) and indicate improved stability for VGOS under increased solar activity, highlighting its potential for more robust ionospheric monitoring during disturbed conditions.

03Projects & Theses

A Shared Deep Feature Embedding of Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 for Building Detection

Master thesis · ETH Zürich · 2023

+3.1 pts IoU on validation from Stable Diffusion VAE features as extra input

Joint deep learning framework for building segmentation using SAR (Sentinel-1) and optical (Sentinel-2) imagery. The work explores domain consistency through adversarial learning and input augmentation, enabling improved single-domain inference while leveraging multi-sensor information during training.

Thesis (PDF)

Vegetation Mapping with ICESat-2

Master project · ETH Zürich · 2022

7.6 → 6.0 m mean canopy-height RMSE against ICESat-2 in seven regions worldwide

Evaluation and bias correction of a global canopy height map using ICESat-2 laser altimetry data across multiple test regions. Systematic over- and underestimation effects were analyzed and mitigated using tile-wise mean corrections and spatial smoothing.

Report (PDF)

Classification of Solid and Loose Rock on Orthophotos with Deep Learning

Bachelor thesis · ETH Zürich · 2021

84.5% test accuracy, ±0.9% across five cross-validation models

Deep learning–based classification of solid rock and loose material in aerial imagery for permafrost-related mapping applications. Implemented with a U-Net architecture and transfer learning, evaluated via cross-validation and data augmentation.
